Aura Privacy Policy

Aura — Virtual Try-On (Chrome Extension) · Effective 27 September 2026

Aura lets you save clothes from any fashion website and see them on an AI-generated avatar of yourself. Doing that requires a photo of you, so this policy explains plainly what we collect, where it goes, who else sees it, and how to get rid of it.

The short version. We collect your name and email from Google sign-in, the photo you upload to create your avatar, the clothing items you choose to save, and a log of your try-ons. Your photo is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate your avatar and is not kept afterwards. We do not track your browsing, we do not sell your data, and we show no advertising. You can delete everything from inside the extension at any time.

Who we are

Aura is an independent project built and operated by Aakanksha Singh. For any privacy question, or to request your data or its deletion, email aakankshagyan3010@gmail.com.

About your photograph

This is the most sensitive thing Aura handles, so we are specific about it.

  • Your photo is uploaded only when you choose to create an avatar. Nothing is captured automatically, and Aura never accesses your camera.
  • It is sent to Google's Gemini API, which generates several candidate avatars. You pick one.
  • We do not store your original photograph. Only the AI-generated avatar you select is saved. The candidates you did not pick are deleted.
  • When you run a try-on, your avatar and the garment images are sent to Google's Gemini API. If that fails, they may instead be sent to fal.ai, which runs the FASHN try-on model as a fallback.
  • Your avatar and try-on images are kept in private storage. They are not publicly listable, and are served only through short-lived links issued to your signed-in session.

What we do not do

  • We do not track your browsing. Aura can run on any website because clothes are sold on many websites — but it reads a page only at the moment you click Scan or use the right-click menu. It does not run in the background, does not record the pages you visit, and sends nothing anywhere unless you ask it to save an item.
  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone, for any purpose.
  • We show no advertising and use no advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We never see your card details. Payments happen on Razorpay's own hosted page; we receive only the amount, the status and a reference number.
  • We do not use your photos or avatars to train AI models of our own.

Who else processes your data

Aura is a small project built on other companies' infrastructure. These are all of them:

  • Google — sign-in, and the Gemini API that generates your avatar and try-ons. See the Gemini API terms and Google Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase — our database, file storage and authentication. See the Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • fal.ai — the fallback try-on model, used only when the primary one fails. See the fal.ai Privacy Policy.
  • Razorpay — payment processing, only if you buy a credit pack. See the Razorpay Privacy Policy.

Where your data is stored

Your account data and images are stored on Supabase infrastructure hosted in Seoul, South Korea. Our AI processing partners may process your images in other countries. If you are using Aura from elsewhere, including India or the EU, your data is transferred and processed outside your country.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, avatar, wardrobe and saved looks are kept until you delete them.
  • Activity records are kept while your account exists, and are removed with it.
  • Purchase records may be retained after deletion where we are required to keep them for tax or accounting purposes. These contain an amount and a reference number, not your images.

Your choices and rights

You can do both of these yourself, from Settings inside the extension:

  • See what we hold. A summary of your account, avatar, wardrobe size and purchase history.
  • Delete everything. This removes your account, your avatar, every image, your wardrobe, your saved looks and your activity records. It is immediate and cannot be undone.

You can also email aakankshagyan3010@gmail.com and we will do it for you. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights — to access, correct, export or restrict the use of your data, or to complain to a data protection authority. We honour those requests regardless of where you live.

You can also revoke Aura's access to your Google account at any time from your Google account permissions. That stops future sign-ins but does not delete data we already hold — use the deletion option for that.

Children

Aura is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Image storage is private by default and access is restricted per account at the database level, so one account cannot read another's files. Traffic is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, and we will not claim otherwise — but your photos are not public, not shared, and not for sale.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that materially affects you, we will update the effective date above and note the change in the extension before it takes effect.
